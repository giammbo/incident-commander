How to read these metrics

MTTR is the mean time from incident creation to its recorded closure, across currently closed incidents created in this window with valid closure timestamps. It measures time to closure, not time to service restoration. Sample: {{ data.mttr_count }} incidents. A dash means there are no eligible closures.

Breakdowns use the same creation cohort ({{ data.total }} incidents). Team attribution uses the current owner of the incident's system, not the responders or the owner at the time of the incident. Unassigned records appear as “—”.

Still open and now closed describe the current state of this cohort. Current backlog above includes older incidents and is independent of the selected window.
